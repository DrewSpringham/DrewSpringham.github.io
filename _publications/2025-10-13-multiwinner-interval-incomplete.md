We study multiwinner approval elections where voters have one-dimensional interval preferences but limited information about candidate positions. Using a probabilistic model with voter groups, we give an algorithm that guarantees Proportional Justified Representation+ (PJR+) using only O(log(σ·k)) preference queries per voter on average, where σ is the number of voter groups and k is the target committee size.
